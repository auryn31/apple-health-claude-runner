Health Markers

Track trends over weeks, not daily fluctuations. A single bad reading means nothing; a consistent shift means something.
Weight
Lose ~0.3 kg/week via mild 300-400 kcal deficit. Don't crash diet. Green band = target zone.
VO2max — aerobic fitness capacity
How much oxygen your body uses (mL/kg/min). Higher = fitter. 45-50 good, 50-55 very good, 55+ excellent for 32M. Estimated from outdoor runs.
Resting Heart Rate — recovery indicator
Lower = stronger heart. Expect 50-65 bpm. A spike of 5+ bpm over baseline = overtraining, illness, or poor sleep.
Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
Variation between heartbeats. Higher = better recovered. 40-60 ms typical, 60+ great. Below 35 ms = take extra rest.
Walking Heart Rate — passive fitness indicator
Your average HR during normal walking. Trends down as you get fitter — no effort required to measure. More reliable than RHR for long-term fitness tracking.
HR Recovery (1 min after exercise)
How many bpm your HR drops in the first minute post-exercise. Higher = better cardiovascular fitness. Under 12 bpm is poor, 20+ is good, 30+ is excellent.
Respiratory Rate — overnight breathing
Breaths per minute during sleep. Normal: 12-20 bpm. A sudden rise can signal illness, stress, or overtraining before you feel symptoms.
Blood Oxygen (SpO2)
Oxygen saturation. Normal: 95-100%. Below 95% is concerning. Useful for spotting illness early. Apple Watch measures overnight.

Running Form & Efficiency

These biomechanics metrics come from the Apple Watch motion sensors during runs. As your form improves: stride length increases, ground contact time decreases, vertical oscillation decreases (less bouncing = more efficient), and power at a given pace drops. Don't obsess over individual readings — look for gradual improvement over weeks.

Sleep Trend

Sleep is when your body repairs and adapts to training. Target 7+ hours per night (green). Consistently under 7 hours raises injury risk, slows recovery, and tanks HRV. Under 6 hours (red) is a training emergency — more sleep will do more for your performance than any workout.
